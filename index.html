About

A product studio,
not an agency

Eonic Labs is an independent technology studio based in Sweden. We don't take on client services work — instead, we concentrate our effort on a small portfolio of products that we design, build and run ourselves. Everything we ship is owned and operated in-house, from the first line of code to the infrastructure it runs on.

Our focus is the digital advertising ecosystem: a complex, fast-moving space where creators, brands, agencies and platforms all meet. We build the software layer that makes those relationships work — automating the repetitive parts, surfacing the data that matters, and letting people spend their time on strategy and creativity.

Ventures

Products we build and operate

Each product in our portfolio is built, operated and supported by the Eonic Labs team — with the engineers who shipped it still behind the wheel.

SpiderAds

Make it smarter, so you can make it smarter.

SpiderAds is an advertising automation platform that handles the full lifecycle of a digital campaign — from creative design through scheduling to performance analysis — with minimal manual work.

  • Create — Design customisable ads with adjustable colours, fonts and layouts. Content is pulled automatically from your website to speed up production.
  • Automate — AutoAds keeps campaigns running 24/7 or on demand, publishing and scheduling without manual intervention.
  • Analyse — Real-time and post-campaign reporting that shows what's working and where to invest next.
  • Integrate — Integration+ and white-label options for agencies that want to embed SpiderAds into their own offering.
Visit spiderads.io →

Influeri

Increase ROI with influencers and UGC by chatting with AI.

Influeri is an AI-powered platform that automates influencer and user-generated content campaigns end-to-end — connecting brands and creators, handling outreach, contracts and payments, and reporting on results.

  • AI matchmaking — Access to 250M+ social profiles, with AI suggesting the right creators for each brand, niche and audience.
  • Campaign creation — Set KPIs, guidelines and budgets in minutes, with AI assistance through every step.
  • Automated operations — Outreach, negotiation, contracting, payments and compliance handled for you.
  • Trusted by 1,000+ brands — Including Segway, Motorola, Alibaba and Wolt.
Visit influeri.com →
