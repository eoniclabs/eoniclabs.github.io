About

A product studio,
not an agency.

Eonic Labs is an independent technology studio based in Sweden. We don't take on client services work — instead, we concentrate our effort on a small portfolio of products that we design, build and run ourselves. Everything we ship is owned and operated in-house, from the first line of code to the infrastructure it runs on.

Our focus is the digital advertising ecosystem: a complex, fast-moving space where creators, brands, agencies and platforms all meet. We build the software layer that makes those relationships work — automating the repetitive parts, surfacing the data that matters, and letting people spend their time on strategy and creativity.

Principles

How we make decisions.

A handful of simple beliefs that shape what we build, who we hire, and what we say no to.

01 — Product

Product over projects

We invest long-term in a small number of products we believe in, rather than chasing short-term consulting engagements.

02 — Engineering

Engineering-led

Our team is built around senior full-stack engineers who have shipped large-scale production systems across adtech, fintech and SaaS.

03 — AI

AI where it matters

We use AI to remove friction from real workflows — not as a buzzword. Every model in production is measured, monitored and accountable.

04 — Sweden

Built in Sweden

Headquartered in Sweden, operating across Europe. We care about privacy, craft, and software that lasts.
