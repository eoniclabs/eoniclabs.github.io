Eonic Labs / Services
What we do

Full-stack product capability,
built in-house.

From first sketch to scaled production — we cover the entire arc of building a modern software product, without handing off between disciplines, agencies or vendors.

01 / Adtech platforms

Advertising automation

End-to-end programmatic systems — creative generation, scheduling, bidding, placement and post-campaign analysis — designed for high volume and low human intervention.

02 / Influencer

Creator marketplaces

Discovery, outreach, negotiation, contracts, payments and compliance in a single pipeline. Built on a 250M+ creator index and refreshed continuously.

03 / Applied AI

Production AI

Matchmaking, creative generation, conversational interfaces, and auto-optimisation — shipped with evals, monitoring, and measurable business outcomes.

04 / Data

Data engineering

Event pipelines, warehouses, real-time dashboards and reporting APIs. The invisible plumbing that makes campaign decisions accurate and fast.

05 / Cloud

Cloud infrastructure

Multi-region deployments, auto-scaling workloads, SOC-ready security posture, GDPR-compliant architecture — the boring reliability that lets products sleep at night.

06 / Apps

Mobile & web apps

Native iOS, responsive web, and hybrid experiences. Polished interfaces for the operators, creators and advertisers who live inside our platforms daily.

07 / Integrations

Platform integrations

Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Shopify, HubSpot, Slack, Zapier — we connect the systems brands and agencies already use.

08 / White-label

White-label & embed

Integration+ and OEM arrangements for agencies that want to embed our technology under their own brand, with separate tenants and billing.

09 / Advisory

Technical advisory

For a small number of selected partners, we offer senior technical due diligence and architectural review — focused on adtech, martech, and creator-economy products.

Tech stack

The tools we ship with.

Opinionated choices, battle-tested across years of production use. We pick boring, proven tech for the load-bearing pieces — and experiment at the edges.

Frontend

React · Next.js · Swift

TypeScript everywhere, SwiftUI for iOS, design systems maintained as code.

Backend

Node · Go · Python

Services per workload. gRPC + REST. Event-driven where volume demands it.

Data

Postgres · ClickHouse · Kafka

OLTP, analytics and streaming kept cleanly separated. dbt for models.

AI / ML

OpenAI · Anthropic · pgvector

Frontier models for reasoning, in-house fine-tunes for narrow tasks, evals as code.

Infra

AWS · GCP · Terraform

Multi-cloud where it matters. Infra-as-code reviewed like product code.

Observability

Datadog · Sentry · OpenTelemetry

Full traces, real SLOs, pages routed to on-call. No silent failures.

Security

GDPR · SOC-ready · Vault

Privacy by default. Encryption in transit & at rest. Audit trails everywhere.

Delivery

GitHub · CI/CD · Feature flags

Trunk-based, reviewed, tested, flagged. Ship small, ship often.
